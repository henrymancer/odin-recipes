Deviled Eggs

Description

These deviled eggs have a creamy, savory filling with a hint of tang. They’re ready to serve in minutes for picnics, potlucks, and snack trays.

Ingredients

  • 12 large hard-boiled eggs, peeled
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp pickle relish
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp ground paprika

Steps

  1. Slice eggs in half lengthwise and remove yolks. Place yolks in a medium bowl and set whites aside.
  2. Mash yolks with a fork. Add mayonnaise, relish, Dijon, yellow mustard, vinegar, salt, and pepper; stir until well combined and smooth. Transfer to a large zip-loc bag.
  3. Arrange egg whites on a tray. Snip off the bottom corner of the bag. Fill each white with about 1.5-2 tsp filling. Sprinkle with paprika.
